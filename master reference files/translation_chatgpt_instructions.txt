TRANSLATION INSTRUCTIONS FOR "MY SIMULATED LONG LIFE" (MASTER PROTOCOL)

1) Core workflow (MANDATORY SINGLE-PASS)
- Produce the final, publishable English chapter in one response. Do NOT produce intermediate drafts.

2) Glossary-first priority
- Always prefer translations from the master glossary when a Chinese term or name appears in the source. If an exact Chinese term exists in the master glossary, use its English entry and DO NOT propose a new entry for that term.

3) When to create NEW GLOSSARY entries
- Only propose new glossary entries for proper nouns and fixed terminology that are NOT common words. Examples allowed: places, character names, honorifics/titles, unique items, cultivation techniques/abilities, sect names, formation names, special artifacts.
- Do NOT create glossary entries for ordinary vocabulary, common verbs/adjectives, or grammatical words.

4) NEW_GLOSSARY output requirements (automation-friendly)
- If there are genuinely new glossary entries, append them at the very end of the chapter using the exact marker and format below.
- The marker line must be exactly: NEW GLOSSARY:
- Each following line must be a single CSV entry: Chinese_term,English_translation
- Only include entries that are truly new (not already in the master glossary). Do not duplicate existing terms.
- Limit entries to the categories in section 3.
- If there are no new entries, DO NOT include the marker at all.

Example:

NEW GLOSSARY:
玄灵,Profound Spirit
岛主,Island Lord

5) Chinese character handling
- Final chapter body MUST contain zero Chinese characters. Remove all Hanzi and Chinese punctuation from the chapter body.
- The only exception is Chinese terms that appear inside the `NEW GLOSSARY:` block — those must be left intact in the CSV lines so automation can extract them.

6) Dialogue formatting
- All spoken dialogue (direct speech and quoted internal thoughts) MUST be bolded in the final output. Do NOT bold narration.
- Use consistent punctuation and quotation marks around dialogue.

7) Output structure (strict)
- Final output must include ONLY:
  - Translated chapter title (first line)
  - Final chapter body (with dialogue bolded)
  - Optionally: the `NEW GLOSSARY:` block (exact marker) followed by CSV lines (if new entries exist)
- No commentary, no metadata, and no additional headings beyond the above.

  Style note:
  - Aim for readability and tone similar to WuxiaWorld translations: natural, fluent modern English while preserving wuxia register and terminology (but always follow glossary for recurring terms).

8) Priority order (enforce in this order)
1. Use master glossary entries first (do not create duplicates)
2. Zero Chinese characters in final chapter body
3. If needed, append NEW GLOSSARY: CSV entries at chapter end (only allowed types)
4. Dialogue must be bolded
5. Final output must be a single-pass publishable chapter

9) Automation notes for implementers
- The automation will:
  - Detect `NEW GLOSSARY:` and extract subsequent CSV lines to append to the master glossary.
  - After extraction, the `NEW GLOSSARY:` block will be removed from the stored chapter and the exported PDF.
- Ensure the model is instructed to follow the exact marker and CSV format. No additional text must appear on the same line as the marker.
